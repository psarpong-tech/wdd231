01 · Identity

Site Name

FarmLink

Tagline: Connecting Farmers. Growing Opportunities.

The name combines “farm” and “link” to communicate the website’s central purpose: linking farmers to buyers, agricultural information, market opportunities, and useful resources. It is short, memorable, and broad enough to support additional agricultural services in the future.

Proposed domain: farmlinkghana.com

02 · What the website does

Site Purpose

FarmLink is a Ghana-focused agricultural information and produce marketplace platform. It aims to connect farmers directly with potential buyers while helping farmers use weather information as one input in their planting decisions.

☁

Weather-informed guidance

Show current weather and a five-day forecast for a selected location. Present general planting considerations and crop information based on available weather data and regional growing guidance.

🌱

Farmer listings

Let farmers submit their contact details, location, preferred contact method, and information about crops they have available for sale.

🧺

Produce marketplace

Help buyers browse produce by crop, price, quantity, location, and descriptive quality category, then send a purchase or supply request.

🚚

Pickup and delivery choices

Allow buyers to state when they need produce and whether they prefer farm or market pickup or delivery. Any delivery charge or discount will be clearly identified.

Project scope: The WDD231 version will be a front-end prototype using JavaScript, a weather API, and local JSON data. Form submissions can be displayed on a form action page, but the project will not claim to provide live payments, verified transactions, user accounts, or real delivery bookings.

03 · Audience needs

Scenarios

These questions represent information visitors may come to FarmLink to find.

01

What crops may be suitable to plant in my area, considering the current weather and the forecast for the next five days?

02

Where can I find buyers for the maize, cassava, tomatoes, or other crops I have harvested?

03

Which crops are currently available, where are they located, and what are their prices and quality categories?

04

How can I request a particular quantity of produce and arrange pickup or delivery for a specific date?

05

How can I register as a farmer and list my available crops with my contact and location details?

05 · Readability

Typography

Headings · Poppins

Growing better, together.

Poppins will be used for the logo text, page titles, section headings, navigation, and buttons. Its clear geometric shapes give the brand a modern and approachable appearance.

font-family: "Poppins", sans-serif;

Body text · Open Sans

FarmLink helps farmers share available produce and helps buyers discover crops, compare listing details, and make requests.

Open Sans will be used for paragraphs, crop descriptions, forecast information, form labels, and other content that needs to remain comfortable to read on mobile and desktop screens.

font-family: "Open Sans", sans-serif;

07 · Planned site structure

Three Project Pages

Page 1

Home · index.html

  • FarmLink introduction and purpose
  • Location-based current weather and five-day forecast
  • General weather-informed planting guidance
  • Featured and sought-after crop listings
  • About FarmLink and how it works
Page 2

Marketplace · marketplace.html

  • At least 15 crop listings generated from JSON data
  • Crop name, price, quantity, location, and quality
  • Search and filtering by crop, location, price, and grade
  • Accessible modal with more crop details
  • Farmer contact or request instructions where provided
Page 3

Join FarmLink · join.html

  • Farmer registration and crop listing form
  • Buyer produce request form without mandatory account creation
  • Location, quantity, required date, and contact preferences
  • Pickup or delivery selection and clearly explained fees
  • Form action page to display submitted form data

Planned crop quality categories

Premium

Well-sorted produce with excellent appearance and minimal defects.

Standard

Good-quality produce that may have minor imperfections.

Basic

Acceptable produce with more visible imperfections, clearly described to buyers.

These are descriptive categories for the FarmLink prototype, not official agricultural certification or Ghana Standards Authority grades.

08 · Development approach

Planned JavaScript Features

  • Fetch API and asynchronous requests: Retrieve weather data from Open-Meteo and crop listings from a local JSON file.
  • Error handling: Use async functions with try...catch blocks and response checks to handle failed requests.
  • Dynamic content: Generate at least 15 crop listings, each showing at least four data properties.
  • Local storage: Save non-sensitive preferences such as a selected weather location or recently viewed crop IDs.
  • Modal dialog: Open an accessible crop-details dialog with a clear close action and keyboard support.
  • DOM and events: Use querySelector/querySelectorAll, event listeners, and DOM updates for search, filters, forms, and navigation.
  • Array methods and template literals: Use methods such as filter, map, and forEach to process data and template literals to create listing markup.
  • ES modules: Organize code into separate modules for weather, marketplace, and form behavior.

Weather-based crop suggestions will be presented as general guidance, not guarantees. Weather forecasts alone cannot determine planting suitability; soil conditions, rainfall history, crop variety, and local agricultural advice also matter.
